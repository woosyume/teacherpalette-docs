Built for explaining

If you are looking for a Presentify alternative, start with the session you want to deliver. Do you need a few marks over an app, or will you also enlarge a detail, sketch an idea on a fresh page, and leave students with an annotated PDF?

TeacherPalette is designed around that wider flow. Beyond Annotation. Presence. Here, presence means making your explanation easy to follow: direct attention, reveal a detail, build the idea, and keep a useful copy.

  1. Screen AnnotationCircle a value, underline a phrase, or connect ideas over the app you are showing.
  2. Spotlight / MagnifierFocus the audience on one area or enlarge a detail they need to read.
  3. WhiteboardGive a worked example its own page, then add another as the explanation grows.
  4. PDF workflowImport your material into Whiteboard, annotate page by page, and export your work as a PDF.

This workflow fits teachers, technical instructors, presenters, trainers, developers creating tutorials, and online educators who regularly explain material on screen.

Compare the workflow you need

When choosing between TeacherPalette and Presentify, try the same short explanation in each tool: mark a slide, navigate an app, make a small detail readable, and work through a question. Notice how comfortably you can move between those tasks.
