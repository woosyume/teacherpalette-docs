Whiteboard for presentations on Mac

A good live explanation often starts as a rough sketch. The useful part is being able to return to it instead of drawing the same thing again next week.

Short answerUse a whiteboard that lets you move between pages, save a session, and bring existing material into the explanation when needed.

Give each idea its own page

Use separate pages for a concept, a worked example, and a question from the room. A blank, grid, lined, or Cornell Notes page gives you a sensible starting point, while Chalkboard mode works well when a dark canvas suits the room.

Reuse the work that still matters

TeacherPalette saves Whiteboard sessions automatically. Reopen a past session from Session History when an explanation deserves another run, rather than redrawing it from memory.

Bring a PDF into the conversation

Load a PDF directly onto the Whiteboard canvas when the discussion begins with a worksheet, handout, or slide export. Annotate above it, then export or print when the material is ready to share.
