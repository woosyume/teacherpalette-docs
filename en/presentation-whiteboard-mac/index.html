Whiteboard for presentations on Mac

When a live explanation needs more room, open Whiteboard and use multiple pages to develop the idea.

Short answerOpen Whiteboard when a quick mark is not enough. Move between pages to separate examples, compare options, and let the explanation grow without crowding the screen.

Use multiple pages to give the idea room

A page can hold a sketch, an example, a question, or a conclusion. Move back and forth while you explain so the audience can see how the pieces connect.

Keep the whole train of thought visible

Save the session when you are done. The next time the topic comes up, your pages are ready to extend instead of starting from a blank canvas.
