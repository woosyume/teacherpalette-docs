How to Use a Whiteboard for Presentations on Mac

Need room for a diagram during a Mac presentation? A multi-page whiteboard lets you separate examples and build an explanation without crowding one canvas.

Short answerOpen TeacherPalette’s Whiteboard, give each idea its own page, and move between pages as you present. Save the session to reuse or extend the explanation later.

Organize your presentation across whiteboard pages

A page can hold a sketch, an example, a question, or a conclusion. Move back and forth while you explain so the audience can see how the pieces connect.

Keep the whole train of thought visible

Save the session when you are done. The next time the topic comes up, your pages are ready to extend instead of starting from a blank canvas.
