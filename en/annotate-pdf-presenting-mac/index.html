How to Annotate a PDF While Presenting on Mac

Need to annotate a PDF while presenting on Mac? Load the document into a whiteboard, then write, highlight or add arrows on the page you are explaining.

Short answerIn TeacherPalette, use Whiteboard’s PDF loading action before the presentation. Move between pages as you explain, keeping enough of the original material visible for context.

Import your PDF before the presentation

In TeacherPalette Whiteboard, use the PDF loading action to bring a PDF onto the canvas. It is not a drag-and-drop workflow. Once loaded, move between pages and write, highlight, or draw arrows over the material as you explain.

Use the right mark for the job

Underline a phrase with a highlighter, box an entire region with a rectangle, and use an arrow when the audience needs to connect two parts of a page. For small text, a rectangular magnifier can show detail while preserving the surrounding document.

Keep a useful copy afterward

When the presentation is finished, export or print the annotated PDF. The writing remains layered over the original material, making it useful as a follow-up handout instead of a disposable live artifact.
